Meet Shameer

Built on decisions he had to make.

Brooklyn-born. Army veteran. Entrepreneur. A man who has had to start over more than once.

Shameer's career has never followed a straight line. He served in the U.S. Army, worked professionally in culinary arts and audio engineering, and went on to build his own business. Each reinvention began the same way: with a decision, and the discipline to act on it.

That experience shapes everything he brings to a stage. He speaks to audiences about the gap between knowing what to do and doing it, and the real cost of waiting. Today he works with entrepreneurs and professionals to bring clarity to their decisions and to execute consistently.

His TEDx talk, Survival Got Better at Hiding, shows how he speaks: personal, direct and practical.

Signature keynote
30 minutes

The Power of One Decision

Most people don't lack ideas or ability. They lack a decision. This keynote shows audiences how a single clear choice, made and acted on, changes performance, leadership and direction, and why the decisions we postpone become the ones that define us.

Audiences leave able to

  • Recognize the decision they have been avoiding
  • Separate real planning from comfortable delay
  • Commit to one clear next action
02 · 45 minutes

The Cost of Indecision

Indecision rarely looks like a problem. It looks like patience, preparation, one more week. This keynote exposes what delay actually costs in opportunity, momentum and confidence, and gives audiences a way to tell the difference between taking time to decide and avoiding the discomfort of deciding.

  • See the hidden price of repeated postponement
  • Ask the question most people miss
  • Move from delay to commitment
